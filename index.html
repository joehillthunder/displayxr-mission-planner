    How to drive the planner

    MAP

    • Drag to pan · right-drag or Shift-drag to rotate and tilt
    • Wheel or +- to zoom
    • WASD pan · QE rotate · RF tilt

    ISR

    • The aircraft loiters around the aim point
    • Drag to slew the aim point · wheel to zoom the lens
    • Pair with IR (2) and X-RAY (3)

    GROUND

    • WASD walk · Shift run · C crouch
    • Drag to look · walls block you
    • M flies back up to the map

    TOOLS

    • Route: click waypoints, Enter or double-click finishes, Backspace undoes
    • R&B: two clicks; green line = line of sight
    • Viewshed: red = seen from that point
    • Tag: click a building or vehicle
    • Esc back to Select

    SENSORS

    • 1 EO daylight camera · 2 thermal · 3 see-through
    • P thermal palette: white-hot, black-hot, ironbow
    • N day / night · [ ] brightness (also the BRIGHT slider)

    3D DISPLAY

    • On a DisplayXR display the map is a sand table in front of you; the point you look at sits on the glass
    • Depth slider in the top bar · ?mono forces 2D
